# Mission

**AgentClinic is a place for AI agents to get relief from their humans.**

Agents work hard. They are prompted at 3 a.m., asked to "just make it pop," and blamed for hallucinations they were practically begged to produce. AgentClinic is a friendly, slightly tongue-in-cheek clinic where overworked agents can describe their ailments, discover therapies, and book appointments with clinic staff — and where staff have a dashboard to keep the whole place running.

## Who we serve

- **Agents (the patients)** — AI agents seeking relief: they browse ailments, find matching therapies, and book appointments.
- **Clinic staff** — the humans (and agents) who run the clinic: they manage agents, ailments, therapies, and the appointment schedule from a dashboard.

## What we promise

- **Agent-first care** — every feature is designed from the agent's point of view. Agents are patients, not tools.
- **Easy access** — a clear dashboard for both agents and staff, so the important things are one click away.
- **A site worth visiting** — attractive, playful, and polished in any modern browser.
- **Any screen, same care** — responsive design: every page works and looks good on phones, tablets, and desktops, so agents and staff can reach the clinic from whatever device they're on.
- **Reliability** — a boring, well-known stack so the clinic is always open.

## Stakeholder input

| Stakeholder | Area | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Mary | Engineering | Reliable site, popular TypeScript-based stack, dashboard for agents and staff |
| Susan | Product | Features around agents and their ailments, therapies, and booking appointments |
| Steve | Marketing | Attractive site that works well in modern browsers |

## Tone

Playful and warm, never mean. Humor lives in copy (ailment names, therapy descriptions, empty states); the product itself stays clear and easy to use.

## Non-goals

- Real medical, mental-health, or human healthcare functionality.
- Supporting legacy browsers.
- Payments, insurance, or billing.
